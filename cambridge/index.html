Heating and Air Conditioning Service Across Cambridge

We service every type of heating and cooling equipment you'll find in local homes and businesses, from central air conditioners and gas furnaces to heat pumps and ductless mini-splits. It doesn't matter whether the building is a single-family house, a townhome, or a busy office. Our techs know the local climate, the age and construction of local homes, and the code requirements that apply to new installations, which pays off in every diagnosis.

Most homeowners we work with need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or a seasonal tune-up. Repairs are usually straightforward: a burned contactor, a dirty flame sensor, a refrigerant leak, or a seized blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling itself to an early death. Maintenance visits cover coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, which does more than anything else to extend equipment life.

Local businesses get the same attention. An overheated storefront or a drafty office drives customers and staff away, so our commercial technicians focus on fast diagnosis and keep high-failure parts on board for rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment. Property managers, restaurant owners, and retailers can count on us to schedule work around their operating hours whenever possible.

Quick Heating and Cooling Help in Cambridge

Two situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can prevent a far bigger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Cambridge

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Minor issues rarely stay small for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
